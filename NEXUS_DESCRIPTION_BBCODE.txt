[size=6][b]Starmancer - Atmosphere, Thermal & Phantom Leak Fix[/b][/size]

A permanent, standalone fix for Starmancer's most notorious game-breaking bugs: the [b]NaN temperature lock[/b], the [b]-273°C cascading room freeze[/b], and [b]phantom air leaks stuck in save files[/b].

[line]

[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]

If you have spent hours building a station in [i]Starmancer[/i], chances are you have encountered one of these game-ending disasters:
[list=1]
[*] [b]The NaN Temperature Bug[/b]: A newly built room, an airlock cycling, or a meteor breach suddenly displays "NaN" for temperature.
[*] [b]The -273°C Cascading Deep Freeze[/b]: Heat exchangers and wall vents connected to a glitched room immediately start draining heat from healthy rooms at [b]60°C every second[/b], freezing your colonists, crops, and machines down past absolute zero.
[*] [b]Phantom Leaks in Save Files[/b]: You patch a hole in the hull with walls and doors, but the game still treats the room as open to the vacuum of space, continuously venting all your air into nothingness.
[/list]

[b]This mod permanently fixes all three issues directly inside the game code.[/b]

Best of all, [b]it automatically heals existing damaged saves[/b]. If your current campaign is frozen or leaking, loading your save with this fix installed will restore your damaged rooms back to a comfortable [b]21°C[/b] within seconds.

[line]

[size=5][b]Main Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*] [b]Fixes the NaN Glitch[/b]: Stops the hidden division-by-zero that corrupts depressurized rooms into NaN.
[*] [b]Stops the -273°C Deep Freeze[/b]: Protects wall vents and heat exchangers so they never drain heat into glitched or unpressurized rooms.
[*] [b]Eliminates Phantom Leaks[/b]: Every 10 leak ticks (~5 seconds), leaking rooms now double-check their surrounding walls. If you sealed the breach, the leak stops immediately and clears itself from the save file.
[*] [b]Auto-Heals Damaged Saves[/b]: You do [b]not[/b] need to start a new game! Loading an existing broken save will safely thaw frozen rooms and repair corrupted tiles back to normal room temperature (21°C).
[*] [b]100% Standalone & Lightweight[/b]: No BepInEx, Doorstop, or mod loaders required. No background scripts or lag—just clean bytecode fixes that run natively with the game.
[*] [b]Steam & Achievement Safe[/b]: Does not interfere with Steam achievements or game stability.
[/list]

[line]

[size=5][b]Installation Instructions[/b][/size]

Choose whichever installation method is easiest for you:

[size=4][b]Option 1: Automated 1-Click Installer (Recommended)[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*] Download the main mod ZIP from the [b]Files[/b] tab and extract it anywhere (e.g. your Desktop or Downloads folder).
[*] Double-click [b]apply-fix.bat[/b].
[*] The installer will automatically locate your Starmancer folder, create a safety backup of your original file ([i]StarmancerMod.dll.orig[/i]), and patch the game in under 2 seconds.
[*] Launch the game and enjoy your station!
[/list]

[size=4][b]Option 2: Manual Drag-and-Drop[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*] In Steam, right-click [b]Starmancer[/b] in your library -> [b]Manage[/b] -> [b]Browse local files[/b].
[*] Go into: [b]Starmancer\Starmancer_Data\Managed\[/b]
[*] Rename the existing [b]StarmancerMod.dll[/b] to [b]StarmancerMod.dll.orig[/b] (this is your vanilla backup).
[*] From the downloaded ZIP, open the [b]prepatched/[/b] folder (or download the manual zip) and copy [b]StarmancerMod.dll[/b] into [b]Starmancer_Data\Managed\[/b].
[*] Launch the game!
[/list]

[size=5][b]How to Uninstall[/b][/size]

You can revert back to 100% vanilla at any time:
[list]
[*] [b]If you used the installer[/b]: Double-click [b]restore-vanilla.bat[/b].
[*] [b]Manual[/b]: Delete [i]StarmancerMod.dll[/i] and rename [i]StarmancerMod.dll.orig[/i] back to [i]StarmancerMod.dll[/i].
[*] [b]Steam[/b]: Right-click Starmancer in Steam -> [b]Properties[/b] -> [b]Installed Files[/b] -> [b]Verify integrity of game files[/b].
[/list]
